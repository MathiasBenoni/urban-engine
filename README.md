This project is about driving a car down a road, and stopping for the traffic lights. You can only steer left / right and you can brake.

# Urban engine

## How to play

- Play by **braking** at the right time, to _avoid_ driving a **red light**
- **Steer** your way around the cars coming at you
- Wait for **green light** before you drive further
- Finish levels

## Controls

- Brake
- Steer
